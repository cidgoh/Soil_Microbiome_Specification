# Domains

The Soil Microbiome Specification organizes fields into thematic domains. Each domain groups fields that describe a related aspect of sample context, collection, processing, or analysis.

The current domains are:

| Domain | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Database Identifiers | Identifiers assigned to samples, sites, projects, and sequences across databases and organizations |
| Sample Collection and Processing | Information about how, when, and where samples were collected, stored, and processed |
| Soil Amendment and Treatment History | Records of amendments or treatments applied to soil prior to sampling |
| Environmental Conditions and Measurements | Environmental measurements taken at or near the time of sampling, including weather and soil properties |
| Soil Nutrients and Chemicals | Chemical and nutrient composition of the soil |
| Strain and Isolation Information | Details about organisms isolated from the sample and the isolation procedures used |
| Sequence Information | Library preparation, sequencing platform, protocol, and file identifiers |
| Bioinformatics and QC Metrics | Quality control assessments and bioinformatics processing details |
| Taxonomic Identification Information | Taxonomic classification results and diversity metrics |
| Risk Assessment Information | Prevalence metrics and experimental intervention details relevant to risk assessment |
| Pathogen Diagnostic Testing | Diagnostic test results for targeted pathogens or assay targets |
| Contributor Acknowledgement | Names of individuals contributing to sample collection, sequencing, analysis, and submission |

> **Note:** The domain structure and field assignments are under active development and may change as the specification is refined.

## How domains are used

Domains are used to organize fields in the specification file. They are not a required submission structure; you do not need to group or submit data by domain.
